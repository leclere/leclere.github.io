Research interests
======
* **Theory**: Multistage Stochastic Programming, Data-driven optimization, Decision focused learning, Decomposition methods, Dynamic programming, Convex optimization
* **Applications**: Energy, SmartGrid, Microgrid, Supply chain, Transportation, Machine learning
